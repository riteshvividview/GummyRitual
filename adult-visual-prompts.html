How Adults differs from Kids

Kids (existing)

Bright, saturated, playful bears, 4 scroll-synced frame-sequence videos in the hero, bouncy motion, Mochibop display face, orange and pink gradients.

Adults (new)

Same closed-studio photography, but a different world: stone, paper, ceramic and glass on muted seamless backdrops. Studio light is the motion. The hero changes lighting colour per product instead of playing video sequences. Gloock serif with spaced Jost capitals. Slower, fewer elements, more air.

Typography

Display: Gloock (Google Fonts, free) — the closest match to “Better Habits. A Healthier You.” in your reference. Tight tracking (-0.02em), line-height around 0.95.

Eyebrows / labels: Jost 400, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.3em. Body: Jost 300.

Studio rules (already written into every prompt)

  • Closed studio only. Seamless paper sweeps, plaster flats, stone and ceramic props. No windows, rooms, sky or outdoors.
  • No plants of any kind. No leaves, sprigs, flowers, branches or leaf shadows. Fruit appears only as clean cut pieces with no stems or leaves.
  • No light effects. No sun rays, light beams, window shadows, gobos, haze, lens flare or glowing rims. Light comes from large softboxes, so shadows are soft and grounded.
  • Real camera, real material: medium-format digital, true-to-life colour, fine stone pores, paper fibre, tiny dust specks, slight imperfections in the gummies.
  • Gummies are sugar-free: matte, lightly powdered, never glossy or glassy.
  • Never let the model write label text. Attach the product crop (see below).

Negative prompt (paste in every time if your tool has a field for it):

plants, leaves, foliage, flowers, branches, herbs, leaf shadows, window, window light, window shadow, sun rays, light beams, god rays, haze, fog, lens flare, glow, bokeh balls, outdoors, sky, landscape, text, letters, watermark, logo, cgi, 3d render, plastic, glossy, oversaturated, hdr, gradient background, floating objects, levitation, clutter, symmetrical face, extra fingers, cartoon

Getting clean transparent PNGs

  • Generators can't output real transparency. Every PNG prompt renders on flat light grey (#d9d9d9) — it separates far more cleanly than white, especially around powder, crystals and glass.
  • Remove the background with Photoshop → Remove Background (best), or Higgsfield / remove.bg on the highest quality setting.
  • Check the edges at 200% on a dark and a coloured background. Red, yellow or grey fringing = re-run the removal, or in Photoshop use Select and Mask → Decontaminate Colors.
  • Export PNG-24 with transparency, at the size in the prompt. Clear (glass, gel) objects: keep their soft shadow off — I add shadows in CSS.

Section 01 · Hero

The Light Dial

Concept: One studio set, one camera position, five lighting setups. A single tin stands on a travertine block in front of a plaster flat. As you scroll, the studio light changes colour and direction, and each light belongs to one product: warm sand → Multivitamin, blush → Skin, cool sage → Hair, soft teal → Gut Health, deep lavender → Sleep.

Build: 5 photographs from the exact same camera position, each with its own product in the shot, crossfaded by scroll (pinned, GSAP). The tin lifts slightly as it swaps. A small circular dial (06:00 → 22:00) shows progress and is the hero's signature interaction. Headline in Gloock: “Better habits. A healthier you.”

Mobile: no pin; plates crossfade every 4 s, tap the dial to change. Reduced motion: the first plate only.
