Studio rules (already written into every prompt)

  • Closed studio only. Seamless paper sweeps, plaster flats, stone and ceramic props. No windows, rooms, sky or outdoors.
  • No plants of any kind. No leaves, sprigs, flowers, branches or leaf shadows. Fruit appears only as clean cut pieces with no stems, crowns or leaves.
  • No light effects. No sun rays, light beams, window shadows, gobos, haze, lens flare or glowing rims. Light comes from large softboxes, so shadows are soft and grounded.
  • Real camera, real material: medium-format digital, true-to-life colour, fine stone pores, tiny dust specks, slight imperfections in the gummies.
  • Pouches are soft-touch matte film with gentle natural creases at the shoulders, a crisp zip seal near the top and a flat gusset base, standing upright on their own.
  • Gummies are sugar-free: matte, lightly powdered, never glossy or glassy.
  • Never let the model write label text. Attach the pouch reference (see below).

Getting clean transparent PNGs

  • Generators can't output real transparency. Every PNG prompt renders on flat light grey (#d9d9d9). It separates far more cleanly than white, which matters here because the pouch tops are white.
  • Remove the background with Photoshop → Remove Background (best), or Higgsfield / remove.bg on the highest quality setting.
  • Check the edges at 200% on a dark and a coloured background. If the white pouch edge looks grey or chewed, re-run the removal, or in Photoshop use Select and Mask → Decontaminate Colors.
  • Export PNG-24 with transparency, at the size in the prompt. Leave the cast shadow out; shadows are added in CSS.
